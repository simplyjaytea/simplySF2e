# T-AUDIT-A: Generator window, anti-slop audit

Scope: 40 `generator-*` fixtures in `tools/ui-preview/out/`. I opened every fixture at 720 or 460, and most at both. I opened the `@460-short` render for busy-progress, error, monster-preview, encounter-preview, character-preview and reskin-relevel-open. Near-duplicates (jev-key, jev-typesafe, jev-off, two-connections and the readiness variants) were checked at one width each, because they differ only in the status bar.

Sources read: `templates/generator.hbs`, `templates/_progress.hbs`, `styles/apps/generator.css`, `styles/simplysf2e.css`, the Generator, Mode, Preview, Relevel, Recent, Tokens, Progress, Party, StatCheck, Skills, BugReport and Presets strings in `lang/en.json`, and `fixtures/generator.mjs`.

Contrast: computed by script from the token values (WCAG relative luminance, with alpha composited over `#0c1620`). No colour picker was run on the PNGs.

## 2. Already right (do not regress)

1. When a creature, encounter, PC or reskin preview exists, Generate drops to `spf-secondary` and reads "Generate another version". The preview card then has exactly one primary (Create…).
2. A dropped or unmatched pick shows strikethrough plus a red `fa-circle-exclamation` with `aria-label`/`title`, so colour is never the only cue. A matched pick shows a green check with an aria-label.
3. The encounter budget chips carry visible "XP"/"Credits" labels. When over budget they add an icon and screen-reader "(over budget)" text. A skipped member keeps its contrast through a dashed border and a "Skipped" word instead of opacity.
4. Re-level table: the Stat/Scale/old/new columns, right-aligned mono numbers that never wrap, the new value in bold as well as colour, and Strike groups with a heading row. The "kept at the old level" line sits outside the `<details>` with an icon and a reason.
5. Token usage collapses, but its `<summary>` always shows the total and the cost line, including the "at least" or ≈ qualifier. The statusbar also shows the last-run cost.
6. Missing compendium content is not hidden in the collapsed readiness row. It renders as an expanded attention row with `role="status"`, an icon, the missing categories and a gear to fix it (empty-attention).
7. A stat-check `<details>` opens when that creature has problems. Problem rows are bold with a glyph and say what differs ("AC 26 on the sheet, but the preview said 24").
8. The error notice names the cause and the next step ("HTTP 429: rate limit… Try again in a moment") and carries "Copy bug report".
9. Narrow layout uses `@container spf-window` only. At 460 and in every `-short` render each control is reachable by scrolling, and there is no sideways scroll.
10. Accessibility basics hold. Each textarea has a visible label (placeholders are examples only). Icon-only buttons carry `aria-label`, and decorative icons carry `aria-hidden`. Mode radios are real inputs with a `focus-within` ring. The `:focus-visible` outline uses the `--spf-focus` token, and a `prefers-reduced-motion` block exists. Text contrast passes everywhere I computed: muted text is about 10.5:1 on the card, `--spf-danger-fg` about 6.7:1, `--spf-success-fg` about 7.0:1, `.spf-custom` white on green about 5.1:1, and the primary label is at least 4.5:1 at the gradient's lightest stop.

## 3. Cross-fixture consistency notes

- **Three disclosure styles:** `.spf-advanced summary` re-implements the chevron in uppercase accent (generator.css l.320–350); `.spf-disclosure` is the shared chevron; the stat-check `<details>` shows the browser's native ▶. Fold `.spf-advanced` into `.spf-disclosure`.
- **Buttons styled three ways in one footer:** the primary uses `.spf-primary`, while Reroll Loot, Discard, Generate Another, Dismiss report, Open (recent) and Manage Presets have no class and fall back to core styling. Preview Plan and Use my party are `.spf-secondary`. In the harness, Preview Plan looks different from Discard.
- **Two level-stepper layouts:** inline label in Monster/NPC/Character, label stacked above in Encounter ("Party level") and in Reskin ("New level").
- **Abbreviations are handled inconsistently** in the encounter `dl`: AC, HP, Per, Fort and Ref get `title` plus `.spf-sr-only` full names, but "Will" has neither (`generator.hbs` ~l.189). That is harmless today, but it breaks the pattern.
- **Type scale is flat:** `--spf-text-sm` and `--spf-text-xs` are both `0.85rem`, so the app relies on uppercase and letter-spacing for hierarchy (see #8).
- **Spacing scale is off the 4/8 rhythm:** `--spf-space-sm` is 0.35rem (5.6 px), and the CSS also uses literal 0.1/0.15/0.2/0.3/0.4/0.45/1.05rem steps.
- **Same state, different glyph sources:** FA triangle (provider, readiness, relevel kept, review heading, budget chips), Unicode ⚠ (stat-check rows), `fa-regular` circle (progress pending).
- **Reroll position:** abilities show name, dice, tag. Spells and feats show name, rank, match icon, dice.
- **Cost wording:** "≥ $x" in the statusbar, "at least $x" in the token report.
- **Capitalisation of the same word:** "rank 1" in spell rows vs "Rank 1" in loot names (this is data, but it shows side by side).
- **Left-edge state colours:** `--spf-warning` #98503d is about 2.5:1 on the card and `--spf-success` #3a7b59 about 3.0:1 (computed). Each is always paired with an icon, so they are not the only cue, but they are weaker than the `-fg` variants used for text.

## 4. Unverified

- **Typography:** Signika and core font sizes are not loaded in the harness, so I cannot judge final heading weights or the uppercase look in-world.
- **Hover states:** `.spf-primary:hover` with its brightness and drop-shadow, the mode-tile hover, and disclosure hover cannot be seen in a static PNG.
- **Focus ring in Foundry:** whether the 2 px outline shows on notched (`clip-path`) cards and on `.spf-card` children, and whether the mode `focus-within` ring is clipped by the card's clip-path. The harness runs no focus.
- **Tab order:** it matches DOM order on paper, but I did not run it. Whether the hidden mode radios are arrow-key navigable in Foundry is also untested.
- **Tooltips:** truncated names (#14) and the mode hints rely on `data-tooltip`, which needs Foundry JS.
- **Core `.notification.warning/.error/.info` inside an app:** in the harness these render as a black box with a coloured border and no icon. Whether Foundry adds an icon, and the real contrast, are unverified, so the "warning has an icon or word" check rests on the wording alone.
- **Unclassed buttons** (Discard, Reroll Loot, Generate Another, Open, Manage Presets, Authorize): their core-styled boundary contrast is unknown.
- **Advanced options content:** never rendered by any fixture (#29).
- **The 360 px-wide minimum window:** the harness checks only 460 and 360-tall.
- **Reskin drop zone:** it is not focusable and has no keyboard path in the template. PLAYBOOK says an Actors right-click Reskin exists, but I did not verify that it covers keyboard-only use.
- **Live Foundry window chrome:** header gradient and border, how the `max-height: 80vh` clamp behaves.
- **Font Awesome:** every icon in the Generator templates is a Font Awesome 6 Free name (fa-dna, fa-id-badge, fa-crosshairs, fa-user-astronaut, fa-masks-theater, fa-book-atlas, fa-hand-fist, fa-dice-d20, fa-clock-rotate-left and others). No Pro-only glyph was found, and none rendered blank.
- **Contrast method:** all ratios were computed from token values with alpha compositing, not measured with a picker on the PNGs. Glow and gradient areas are estimates.
