# UI audit 2026-11-08 (anti-slop checklist)

Status: **findings for JT, nothing built.** Three Opus auditors ran `.claude/skills/ui-ux/references/anti-slop-checklist.md` over all 94 harness fixtures at 720 px and 460 px (short renders where it mattered) on `next` after BATCH-1. Raw reports with every fixture id: `docs/archive/ui-audit-2026-11-08/` (generator, forge-shop-treasure, setup-windows). Contrast figures are computed from the `--spf-*` token values, not picked from pixels. Typography, hover, tab order and Foundry core chrome cannot be judged in the harness.

Totals: 90 findings; 10 high, 38 medium, 42 low. Most highs are one cause: the shared theme.

## 0. The one decision: the "Neon Drift" theme

`styles/simplysf2e.css` is a deliberate neon HUD kit: magenta gradient primary button with glow and text-shadow, cyan-to-magenta progress fill with a moving sheen, glowing status bar and selected tiles, scan-line textures on empty panels, corner ticks and a notched `clip-path` on every `.spf-card`, a pulsing card border while busy, seven accent colours at once in the Generator (cyan, magenta, five per-mode tints). Every auditor flagged it as checklist §1 (looks like generic AI UI, not Foundry) and §5 (motion beyond Foundry's). It was JT's design choice, so PLAYBOOK §3.7 applies: JT decides before anything in §1 below that touches it.

| Option | What changes | Cost | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Keep the theme | Only the non-theme findings below are built. | none | Shell still fails §1; the rest of the audit still improves copy, hierarchy and accessibility. |
| B. Flatten the shell, keep the palette (**recommended first step**) | Flat token fill on `.spf-primary`, no glow, no text-shadow, no sheen, no scan lines, no corner ticks or notches, no pulse or blink; keep cyan as the one accent and the dark surface scale; per-mode tints reduced to the active tile only or removed. | M, CSS only (`simplysf2e.css`, small edits in `generator.css`, `itemforge.css`, `provider-setup.css`, `welcome.css`, `sources.css`) | Passes §1 and §5 without a redesign; reversible in one commit. |
| C. Retheme to sf2e sheet idiom | B, plus surfaces, borders, trait tags, headings and chips matched to the sf2e NPC sheet (Signika weights, square tags, sheet greys). | L, needs a sheet screenshot reference and a design pass | Looks native next to an sf2e sheet. Can follow B. |

## 5. Already right (do not regress)

- Generate drops to secondary once a preview exists; dropped picks are struck through with a red icon and label, never colour alone.
- Encounter budget chips, re-level tables and the "kept at old level" line are clear and always visible; the run cost is always visible; the token summary shows cost when collapsed.
- Missing compendium content and stat-check problems render expanded; Provider Setup forces the Cost estimate open on a price error with a red edge and "Needs a fix" in text plus icon; the Jev section opens when active.
- Narrow tables fold Type under the name, keep the full label for screen readers under "Lvl", and keep remove buttons aligned.
- Non-colour selected cues everywhere (`aria-pressed`, bold, inset border, accent rail plus checkbox); one shared focus ring covers every control; icon-only buttons are labelled; decorative icons are `aria-hidden`.
- Sources Save/Reset stay pinned while the list scrolls and `scroll-padding` keeps a focused checkbox clear; the filter only hides rows and the status line is `aria-live`.
- Every short render reaches the footer; no sideways scroll at 460; narrow rules use `@container`; reduced motion stops the spinners.
- Text contrast passes 4.5:1 on every computed pair (muted text 10.5:1, accent 9.7:1, danger 6.7:1, success 7.0:1); the primary label sits exactly at 4.50:1 on the gradient top.
- Most copy names cause and next step (NoMatches, PriceInputInvalid, JevCorsWarning, SampleHint, the error states).
